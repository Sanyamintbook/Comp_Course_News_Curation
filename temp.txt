AI-Powered Student Recommendation Platform
1. Purpose 
Build a single AI-powered recommendation platform that automatically understands educational content and delivers personalized recommendations across Courses, Competitive, News, Tests and Leisure based on each student's profile and learning journey.
2. Business Principle
The platform does not depend on manually created metadata. Every new content item is processed by AI so the platform can understand what the content is about before it becomes searchable. The recommendation engine then combines student profile, AI understanding and student behaviour to deliver relevant recommendations.

5. Recommendation Logic
The recommendation engine never asks AI to recommend from the entire repository.

Step 1: Retrieve semantically similar content using embeddings.
Step 2: Apply business rules (student profile, language, educational suitability, module filters).
Step 3: Ask the LLM to rank only the shortlisted candidates.
Step 4: Display the highest ranked recommendations.
6. Personalization Flow
Initial recommendations are based on the onboarding profile.
As students interact with the platform, their behaviour continuously updates their interest profile. Future recommendations improve using:
• Search history
• Clicks
• Watch time
• Completed courses
• Quiz attempts
• Bookmarks
• Favourite topics

7. Dashboard Experience
Each module is personalized:
• Courses – Recommended learning content
• Competitive – Relevant competitions and preparation
• News – Relevant educational news
• Tests – Practice assessments
• Leisure – Educational activities and projects

Selecting a topic (e.g. Programming) automatically groups all related content from every module under the same topic.

9. Future Enhancements
Collaborative filtering, recommendation explanations, adaptive learning paths, administrator review workflow, analytics dashboards and continuous model evaluation.
10. Conclusion
This architecture separates content understanding from recommendation. AI first understands content, then the recommendation engine combines semantic similarity, business rules and student behaviour to deliver personalized educational recommendations. The design is modular, scalable and suitable for an MVP while providing a clear path to enterprise-scale deployment.
